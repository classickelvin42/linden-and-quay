Privacy

This demonstration does not send form submissions to a server. Names, email addresses, phone numbers, and messages stay in the page until you leave or edit them. They are not written to a database.

The site stores one item in session storage: the last listings URL, so “Back to search results” can restore your filters. That item is cleared when the browser session ends. There is no analytics tag and no advertising cookie.

Fonts are files served with this website, not loaded from a third party. Street photographs are credited on the image credits page.

If you later connect a form service, replace this page with a policy that names the provider, the purpose, how long messages are kept, and how someone can ask for theirs to be deleted. The technical hook is the comment at the top of js/forms.js.
