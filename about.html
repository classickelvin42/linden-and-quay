About

A Bristol desk for sales and lettings.

Linden & Quay, in this demonstration, helps people buy, sell, and rent homes in Bristol and in Long Ashton. The copy below explains the service. It is not a record of completed sales, awards, or staff.

Who it is for

The sample catalogue is written for people comparing a first flat, families moving between north and south Bristol, renters, and landlords who want a letting handled locally. Investors can use the same search. This demo does not offer investment advice.

What a sale instruction includes

  1. Valuation visit We agree a time to see the home and talk through comparable sales you care about. This website cannot produce a figure on its own.
  2. Marketing, once instructed Photographs, a floor plan, and a listing price are agreed in writing. None of the photos in the sample catalogue are client photography.
  3. Viewings and offers Enquiries are passed on and viewings are arranged with you. Offers are confirmed in writing. You appoint your own solicitor. This page is not legal advice.

What a letting instruction includes

  1. Rent and terms We suggest a rent to test, the length of the tenancy, and whether bills are included. The sample rents do not settle those questions.
  2. Safety checks Gas, electrical, and other checks required for a let stay with the landlord. This demo does not confirm that any check has been done.
  3. Applications Referencing and the deposit are explained before anyone is asked to pay. The contact form here does not take a payment.

Fees

Fees depend on the instruction and must be given in writing before you sign. This demonstration does not publish percentages, and it does not describe a fee as typical or fixed.

Service area

Bristol: Bedminster, Bishopston, Clifton, Cotham, Easton, Harbourside, Hotwells, Redland, Southville, St Werburgh's, and Totterdown. Also Long Ashton, just into North Somerset. Other places can be discussed; they are not in the sample list.

People

Do not add agent biographies until you have permission to publish names, photographs, and any registration details. This demo leaves that section empty on purpose, rather than inventing a team.
