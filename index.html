How a sample instruction moves

If you are buying

  1. Search the areas you already know.
  2. Open a listing and check the facts that are actually filled in.
  3. Send a viewing request. On this demo it stops in the browser.

If you are renting

  1. Switch the search to rent so prices are per month.
  2. Ignore anything marked let agreed, or hide those with the filter.
  3. Ask what a real tenancy would include. This site does not say.

If you are a landlord

  1. Request a rental valuation.
  2. Agree the rent, the term, and who answers repairs before marketing.
  3. Applicants are referenced by you or your agent. Nothing here is pre-checked.

About this catalogue

What the sample set contains

Prices for sale

The twelve homes run from £275,000 in Long Ashton to £1,450,000 in Cotham. Those numbers describe this file of sample data, not the Bristol market.

Rents

Sample rents are £1,250 to £1,850 per calendar month. The pages do not say whether bills are included.

What is left out

No exact address, council tax band, energy rating, or travel time. Add those only when they come from a real listing.

Thinking of selling or letting?

Book a conversation and say whether you want a sale valuation or a rental figure. This form does not notify anyone.
